ℹ️ Why the 2× buffer matters, beyond a checkbox

If your trip would cost ₦3,000,000 and your account doesn't show at least ₦6,000,000, a reviewer often reads that gap as financial stress — a sign you might struggle to support yourself while abroad, or that you're leaning on this trip in a way that makes your intention to return home less certain. Meeting the ratio isn't about performing wealth; it's about showing you can absorb the trip's cost without it destabilizing your life in Nigeria, which is part of what reassures an officer you're coming back.

    🚫 Documents best avoided as sole evidence

    ▸

    Caseworkers across most visa systems give little weight to these on their own — use them only alongside stronger evidence above: credit card statements (not bank statements), driving licence as identity proof, flight/hotel bookings alone as proof of ties, photocopies where an original is reasonably available, and leisure or short-course certificates unrelated to your visit purpose.
