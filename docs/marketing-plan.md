# Smooth Application — Marketing Plan

*Prepared as a growth/performance marketing plan for a bootstrapped, pre-revenue consumer product in a crowded, regulation-sensitive category. Every channel recommendation below is checked against actual platform policy and Nigeria-specific usage data as of September 2026 — sources are cited throughout, not assumed.*

---

## 1. Positioning statement

**For** Nigerians preparing a visa application (UK first, expanding to South Africa, Ghana, Kenya, Ethiopia, Morocco) **who** are anxious about being refused and don't trust generic checklists, **Smooth Application is** the only visa-prep tool that reads your actual bank statement narration-by-narration and builds your real income story — not a generic AI probability score, but a document-by-document, reason-by-reason case file you can walk into your appointment with. **Unlike** Consular AI, VisaGenes, TravelReady, or Permito, which give you a single opaque score and a generic checklist, Smooth Application shows its work: every flagged transaction, every classification, is explainable and editable by you.

This is the wedge. Own it before trying to out-feature anyone.

---

## 2. Competitive reality check (why this matters for the plan)

This is not a blue ocean. As of this writing, at least five funded-or-funded-adjacent products are already live in the exact same category:

- **Consular AI** — Nigeria-specific, "200,000 real embassy decisions" ML model, free-to-start, sells an approval-probability report.
- **VisaGenes (MetropolVisa)** — UK-only, 0–100 readiness score with sub-scores, backed by an actual licensed visa consultancy, document ingestion beyond bank statements.
- **TravelReady** — 200+ destination coverage, programmatic SEO content engine (see §6), free checklist + paid pre-submission review.
- **Permito** — US-specific, readiness score + paid mock-interview tiers ($19–$59).
- **VisaClear** — markets an "89% success rate" document-analysis angle.

None of them do transaction-level Nigerian bank-narration parsing the way Smooth Application does. That specificity — not "AI visa prep" as a category — is the marketing asset. The plan below is built around making that the whole story, rather than competing head-on with better-funded players on "who has the flashier score."

## 3. Target audience (ICP segmentation)

| Segment | Description | Primary channel fit |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary: The self-preparer ("Japa" seeker)** | 24–40, Lagos/Abuja/PH, white-collar or small-business income, has been refused once or is terrified of being refused, doing this alone without an agent | WhatsApp, TikTok, SEO |
| **Secondary: The referred applicant** | Told by a friend/family member who used it or a consultant who recommends it | WhatsApp referral, word of mouth |
| **Tertiary (B2B2C wedge): Visa agents & consultants** | Independent visa consultants/agents in Lagos who prep multiple clients' bank statements by hand every week | Direct outreach, LinkedIn, WhatsApp Business |
| **Diaspora-adjacent: Family sponsors** | UK/US/Canada-based Nigerians helping a parent/sibling apply, often paying for the trip themselves | Facebook (older demographic), diaspora WhatsApp/Facebook groups |

The tertiary segment (agents/consultants) deserves more weight than a typical B2C plan would give it — see §8.

## 4. Platform reality in Nigeria (grounds every channel decision below)

Verified current data, not assumption:

- **WhatsApp** is used by 96.5% of Nigerian internet users 16+ monthly and is the single favourite platform for 33.9% of users — the largest single channel by a wide margin. [DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-nigeria)
- **TikTok** has overtaken as Nigeria's largest platform by ad reach (47.8M) and is described as the country's "primary cultural driver" in 2026; 66% of young Nigerians use it regularly, 71% of those daily. [Brand Communicator](https://brandcom.ng/2026/02/05/top-trends-2026-tiktok-is-nigerias-largest-social-platform-primary-cultural-driver/)
- **Instagram** usage is high (89.2%) but ad reach is *declining* (-6.1% YoY) — deprioritize paid Instagram spend relative to TikTok.
- **Facebook** remains the #2 favourite platform (29.7%) and skews slightly older — relevant for the "family sponsor" segment.
- **X (Twitter)** is a distant 5th favourite (5.1%) but punches above its weight for the specific "Japa"/relocation discourse niche — worth an organic presence, not ad spend.

Implication: **the plan is WhatsApp- and TikTok-first, not Meta-ads-first** — which also happens to be the compliance-safer path (see §5).

## 5. Compliance — read this before spending a single naira on ads

This is the single most important section in this plan, and it's the one a generic marketing consultant would miss.

**Google Ads restricts this exact category.** Google's "Government Documents and Official Services" policy (tightened October 2026) explicitly covers "immigration assistance, visa facilitation... proof of immigration status/registration and travel documents like visas." To run ads at all, an advertiser's domain must be linked from and explicitly authorized by an actual government website — Smooth Application almost certainly does not qualify, and most independent visa-prep tools don't either. **Do not budget for Google Search/Display ads for visa-related keywords without first confirming certification status; assume it will be rejected.** [TechWyse](https://www.techwyse.com/news/platform-updates/google-ads-government-documents-services-policy-update-october-2026), [Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ads-restricts-government-documents-official-services-29188.html)

**Meta's policy is less explicit but carries real precedent risk** — Meta has faced public scrutiny over immigration-adjacent ad targeting (including government use of Facebook ads to deter migrants), and "immigration consultancy" ads are a known trouble spot on Google Ads' sister policies. Treat Meta ads as *possible but requiring careful, conservative copy* (no probability/outcome claims, no "guaranteed approval" language, clear "not immigration advice" disclosure) — test small before scaling spend, and expect some ad rejections as normal, not a sign to change tactics. [Meta Ad Standards](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/)

**Regulatory framing risk (not just ad-platform risk):** the UK's OISC regulates who may give "immigration advice." Smooth Application must stay unambiguously positioned as a *document-organization and financial-evidence tool*, never as advice on whether someone will be approved or what they should legally do. This should be baked into every piece of marketing copy, not just a disclaimer buried in a footer — competitors like VisaGenes get away with score language partly because they're built inside a licensed consultancy; a solo-built tool doesn't have that cover.

**Net effect on strategy:** paid search/display is largely closed off by policy, not by budget. This pushes the plan toward organic content, creator partnerships, WhatsApp distribution, and direct B2B2C partnerships with licensed consultants — channels that are both cheaper AND lower-risk here. That's a genuine silver lining, not just a constraint.

## 6. Content & SEO strategy — the programmatic play

TravelReady's actual playbook (visible in their live site structure) is programmatic country-pair SEO content: individually-published pages like "Malaysia to Australia Visa: Complete 2026 Guide," "Sri Lanka to Australia Visa," etc. — one templated page per (origin country × destination country × visa type) combination, targeting long-tail search intent at scale.

**Smooth Application should run the same play, narrower and deeper:** one page per (Nigerian state/city × destination country) combination, e.g. "UK Visitor Visa Guide for Lagos Applicants," "UK Visitor Visa Guide for Port Harcourt Applicants," plus per-bank guides — "How Sterling Bank Statements Are Read for UK Visa Applications," "How Zenith Bank Statements Are Read for UK Visa Applications," one per bank already validated in the engine (Sterling, Zenith, Fidelity, First Bank, UBA, Opay). This is content nobody else can credibly write, because nobody else has actually parsed those banks' narration formats. It is also naturally link-bait for Nigerian personal-finance and "Japa" blogs to reference.

Target keyword clusters (validate search volume before committing writer time, but directionally strong given the "32% rejection rate" framing already used by a competitor's own blog):
- "[bank name] statement UK visa"
- "UK visa proof of funds Nigeria"
- "why was my UK visa refused Nigeria"
- "[state] to UK visa requirements"

## 7. Growth loops — where the real leverage is

### 7a. WhatsApp Communities (highest-leverage, free)
WhatsApp Communities are free, used by over 90 million Nigerians daily, and Nigerian businesses using them well report material engagement and conversion lifts. [Busnurd](https://busnurd.com/whatsapp-marketing-strategy-nigerian-businesses/) Build a "Smooth Application — UK Visa Prep" community with:
- A weekly "refusal letter teardown" post (anonymized, with permission) — this is exactly the kind of content that gets forwarded 1:1 in Nigerian WhatsApp culture.
- A referral mechanic: completing your checklist unlocks a shareable "readiness card" (see §7c) with a WhatsApp share button pre-filled with a referral link.
- QR codes on any printed/physical touchpoint (agent offices, if the B2B2C angle below lands) driving straight into the community.

### 7b. Visa agent / consultant partnerships (B2B2C wedge)
Independent Lagos-based visa consultants manually build exactly this kind of income-source narrative for clients today, by hand, in Excel. Smooth Application is a tool they would pay for or white-label to look more professional to their own clients. This is a warmer, cheaper acquisition channel than consumer ads, and it sidesteps the ad-policy problem entirely (B2B outreach isn't restricted the way consumer ad targeting is). Concretely: identify 15–20 visa consultants/agents via Instagram/LinkedIn/WhatsApp Business directories, offer free access in exchange for feedback + referrals, convert the ones who like it into paid seats or a rev-share on referred applicants.

### 7c. Shareable "readiness card" (screenshot bait)
Every competitor gives a punchy single number to screenshot and share. Smooth Application's honest, section-by-section percentages are more useful but weaker share bait. Fix: generate a single shareable image at the end of a session — e.g. "My visa prep is 82% ready — Documents ✅ Finances ⚠️ 2 items left" — styled, brandable, WhatsApp/Instagram-Story-shaped (1080×1920). This gives the product a screenshot-native growth loop without compromising the "we don't give false probability scores" positioning — it's a readiness snapshot, not an approval prediction.

### 7d. Creator partnerships in the "Japa" content niche
"Japa" relocation content is already a major, monetized Nigerian content category on TikTok and YouTube. [ThePIENews](https://thepienews.com/japa-nigerians-study-abroad/) Rather than broad influencer spend, target 5–8 mid-tier (10K–100K) Japa/UK-relocation creators for a seeded, honest walkthrough ("I used this to check my bank statement before my UK visa appointment") — mid-tier creators in this niche typically convert better on trust than top-tier ones on reach, and cost meaningfully less. Structure as a flat fee + affiliate code (trackable in the referral system from §7c), not a pure reach buy.

## 8. 90-day phased plan

**Phase 1 (Weeks 1–4) — Foundation & compliance-safe organic**
- Ship the shareable readiness-card feature (drives §7c/7d loops).
- Publish the first 6 per-bank SEO guides + 3 city-guides.
- Stand up the WhatsApp Community; seed it with your own existing users/testers.
- Draft and legal-review the "not immigration advice" positioning language across every surface (landing page, report output, any future ad copy).
- Identify and reach out to first 10 visa consultants/agents.

**Phase 2 (Weeks 5–8) — Creator seeding & B2B2C conversion**
- Run 3–5 seeded creator partnerships (flat fee + affiliate code).
- Convert first agent partnerships into active users; gather testimonials.
- Expand SEO content to 15+ pages; start tracking organic search impressions.
- Small, conservative Meta ads test (₦50–100K spend) with conservative, disclaimer-forward copy — purely to validate ad account survives review, not to scale yet.

**Phase 3 (Weeks 9–12) — Scale what worked**
- Double down on whichever of (organic SEO / WhatsApp referral / creator / agent channel) shows the best CAC signal from Phase 1–2 — don't split budget evenly, follow the data.
- If Meta ads survived review cleanly, scale spend modestly; if not, redirect that budget into creator/agent channels instead.
- Begin South Africa/Ghana/Kenya-specific content and community segments if UK channel is producing signal.

## 9. KPIs to actually track

Given no ad spend is guaranteed to be viable (§5), vanity "reach" metrics are less useful here than for a typical SaaS plan. Track:

- **WhatsApp Community size + weekly active share rate** (a message being forwarded out of the community is the real growth signal, not just membership count).
- **Readiness-card share rate** (% of completed sessions that generate and share a card).
- **Organic search impressions/clicks per published guide** (validates the SEO bet without needing paid traffic).
- **Agent-referred sessions vs. direct sessions** (tells you whether the B2B2C wedge is working).
- **Cost per activated user by channel**, once any paid spend starts — don't average this across channels, since the policy landscape means channels are not comparable.

---

## 10. Budget framing (bootstrap-appropriate)

No paid-ads-first budget is recommended given §5. A reasonable early allocation, assuming a modest monthly marketing budget:

- **50%** — content production (per-bank/per-city SEO guides, readiness-card design) and creator seeding fees.
- **30%** — B2B2C partnership development (this is largely time/relationship cost, not ad spend — budget for a few in-person/virtual demo calls, possibly light incentive/referral payouts to early agent partners).
- **20%** — small, conservative paid test budget (Meta only, after copy/compliance review), treated as an experiment, not a channel to scale until it's proven to survive ad review and convert.
